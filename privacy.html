Privacy Policy

Wallpaper 4K HD · Effective 13 September 2026

Wallpaper 4K HD ("the app") is published by Questbyte Labs. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, or usage data.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data on your device only, so it works smoothly:

  • Wallpaper catalogue cache — titles, categories and image links, so the grid loads instantly and works offline.
  • Favorites and recently viewed — which wallpapers you have hearted or opened.
  • Settings — grid size, title overlay, and whether new-wallpaper alerts are on.
  • Image cache — downloaded thumbnails and previews.

None of this leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it; you can also clear the image cache, favorites and recents from the app's Settings screen.

Network requests

To show wallpapers, the app downloads the catalogue and images from our content server (hosted on Supabase). These requests carry no personal information — only the standard technical details any internet connection involves, such as your IP address, which the hosting provider may log briefly for security and operation. We do not use this to identify or track you.

Permissions

  • Internet — to download wallpapers.
  • Set wallpaper — to apply an image to your home or lock screen when you ask.
  • Notifications (optional, Android 13+) — to tell you when new wallpapers are added. You can decline or turn this off in Settings at any time.

Downloads are saved to your Pictures/Wallpapers folder through Android's media store, so the app never needs access to your other photos or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and, as it collects no data, does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised.
