Privacy Policy

Virat Kohli Wallpapers 4K HD · Effective 16 November 2026

This policy applies to Virat Kohli Wallpapers 4K HD (Android package com.questbyte.viratkohliwallpapers), published by Questbyte Labs. It explains how the app handles information when you browse, save, share or apply wallpapers.

Information and accounts

The app does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts or precise location. The app has no separate analytics or crash-reporting service. Wallpaper hosting services receive technical information when the app connects, as explained below. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use it to respond to your request.

Wallpaper downloads and hosting

The app requests its wallpaper catalogue from Supabase and downloads images from Cloudflare or Supabase hosting. These providers receive your IP address and ordinary connection and request details to deliver content and operate, secure and troubleshoot their services. We do not upload your favorites, recent viewing history or personal photo library to these services. See Supabase's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy for their data handling practices.

Advertising and privacy choices

Advertising is disabled in the current app build. The Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP) libraries are included. Although the app does not request or display ads while its advertising switch is off, it still requests consent information from Google when the app opens. Google may therefore receive technical connection, device and consent-related information to provide that service. Disabling ad serving does not mean the app makes no connections to Google.

If advertising is enabled in a future release, Google AdMob may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Data handling depends on the enabled SDK features, configuration and your privacy choices. This policy will be updated when those practices change. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosures and Google's privacy policy.

The app uses Google's consent tools to request and manage applicable privacy choices. Where available, Settings → Privacy options lets you reopen Google's privacy form. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. No consent form or particular privacy option is guaranteed to appear in every region or configuration.

Purchases

Optional premium upgrades are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app receives purchase status, product identifiers and purchase tokens from Google Play to acknowledge purchases and unlock or restore premium features on your device. These requests are handled through Google Play Billing. See Google's privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

  • Catalogue and image caches: wallpaper details, thumbnails and previews used for loading and offline access.
  • Favorites and recent wallpapers: the images you save or open.
  • Preferences: display settings and notification choices.

You can clear the image cache, favorites and recent items in the app's Settings. Android's app storage controls let you clear app data. Uninstalling normally removes private app data, but images you downloaded to shared storage remain until you delete them. Some preferences may be backed up or transferred by Android according to your system settings; the app's wallpaper database is excluded from its configured backup and device-transfer rules.

Permissions, saved images and sharing

  • Internet and network state: to load wallpaper catalogues, images and network-dependent features.
  • Set wallpaper: to apply your selected image to the home or lock screen.
  • Notifications and background work: the app checks periodically for new wallpapers and schedules a daily wallpaper reminder. New-wallpaper alerts can be changed in the app. The daily reminder is separate and can be managed through its Android notification channel; turning off new-wallpaper alerts does not turn off the daily reminder. Android notification permission and system settings control whether notifications appear. The app can reschedule its background work after a device restart.

Downloaded wallpapers are saved through Android's MediaStore in Pictures/Wallpapers. The app does not need broad access to your other photos or files. When you choose Share, Android passes the selected wallpaper title and image link to the app you choose; that app's own privacy policy applies.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you clear it or uninstall, subject to Android backup behavior described above. Hosting providers retain technical logs under their own policies; we do not specify or guarantee a particular log-retention period. Support correspondence is kept as needed to address your request. Contact us to request deletion of correspondence or ask about data handling. The app has no user account to delete. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

The app is a general-audience wallpaper app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review and remove it.

Security and changes

The app uses HTTPS for its configured wallpaper services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. We update this policy when app features or data handling change, and revise the effective date shown above.
