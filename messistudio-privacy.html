Privacy Policy

Messi Wallpaper Studio · Effective 8 November 2026

This policy covers Messi Wallpaper Studio, package com.questbyte.messistudio, provided by Questbyte Labs. It describes the current ads-disabled release. The app lets you browse, save, share and apply wallpapers. It does not require an account.

Information handled by the app

The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone access. It does not upload your photo library, favorites, viewing history or search queries to our wallpaper backend. We do not use an analytics or crash-reporting service in this release, sell personal data, or use your activity for targeted advertising.

Wallpaper downloads and hosting

The app retrieves wallpaper records from Supabase and images from our image-hosting infrastructure, including Cloudflare. Searches and category selection operate on the downloaded catalogue. If you enable new-wallpaper alerts, the app periodically checks the catalogue in the background; it does not need a push-notification account or registration token.

Network providers necessarily receive technical connection data such as your IP address, request time and the requested resource. This information is used to deliver content, operate the service and protect it against abuse. Hosting services may retain operational or security logs under their own settings and policies; we do not use those requests to build advertising profiles. Their infrastructure may process requests in countries other than yours.

Provider information: Supabase privacy policy and Cloudflare privacy policy. Connections to the configured wallpaper services use HTTPS.

Advertising and purchases

Ads are disabled in this release. The app does not request or display ads, and its ad-consent flow and premium-purchase entry points are disabled. Google Mobile Ads, consent and billing libraries remain bundled for possible future versions, but the app does not start ad serving or connect to Play Billing in this release. We do not use the advertising ID to serve or personalize ads in this version.

If advertising, purchases or other data practices are enabled in a later release, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before that release. Any required choices or consent will be presented at that time.

Information stored on your device

  • Catalogue and image caches: wallpaper records, thumbnails and previews used for browsing and faster loading.
  • Favorites and recents: wallpapers you save or open.
  • Preferences: grid size and notification settings.
  • Downloads: images you explicitly save to Pictures/Wallpapers.

App-managed data stays on your device unless Android includes eligible preferences or other app data in system backup or device transfer. The catalogue database is excluded by the app's backup rules. Backup availability, retention and restoration depend on your Android settings and backup provider. We do not operate a cloud account or synchronization service for your favorites or history.

Permissions and sharing

  • Internet and network state: download wallpaper content and check connectivity.
  • Set wallpaper: apply a wallpaper when you request it.
  • Notifications: optional alerts for new wallpapers. You may decline permission or disable alerts in the app or Android settings.

Downloads use Android's media store without requiring broad access to your other photos or files. If you choose Share, the app sends the selected wallpaper's title and link to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. That receiving app handles the shared content under its own privacy policy. Opening store or external links similarly uses the selected external service.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Cached images may be removed automatically or cleared through the app's settings. You can clear favorites and recently viewed items in the app, or clear app storage through Android settings. Uninstalling removes the app's private local data, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. Downloaded images and an applied wallpaper can remain after uninstalling; delete images using your gallery or file manager and change the wallpaper in Android settings. Manage any system backups through your backup provider.

There is no app account to delete. For privacy questions or requests concerning information held by us, including information you voluntarily email to support, contact the address below. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Hosting-provider logs are governed by the provider's retention settings and applicable requirements; uninstalling the app does not delete those logs.

Security

We use HTTPS for wallpaper-service connections and Android's application storage protections for private local data. No network or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Wallpaper content

This is an unofficial fan app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lionel Messi, any club, league, federation or sponsor. Names identify the subject of the wallpapers. Rights holders may contact us with requests concerning attribution or removal.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Material changes will be communicated in the app or its store information where appropriate.
