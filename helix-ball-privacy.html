Privacy Policy

Helix Ball 3D: Ultimate Spiral Drop · Effective 3 November 2026

This policy covers the game Helix Ball 3D: Ultimate Spiral Drop (package com.questbytes.helixball), published by Quest Byte Labs ("the game", "we"). The short version: we do not run any servers and do not collect, sell or share your personal information ourselves. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data to show and measure ads, as described below. It has no accounts, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own, and gameplay works offline.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress and preferences, the game keeps the following in a file on your device only:

  • Profile: the player name, avatar and frame you choose, your player level and XP.
  • Progress: reached levels, stars and best scores, boss towers beaten, daily challenge, missions, achievements, daily login rewards, event and season progress, and inbox messages.
  • Coins and items: your coin and gem balances, power-ups, and the balls, trails, effects, sounds, tower themes and environments you own and use.
  • Statistics and records: runs, rings passed, combos, fireballs, play time, recent run history and your own best scores per mode.
  • Settings: volume, music, sound effects, vibration, touch sensitivity, inverted rotation, graphics quality, frame rate, battery saver and accessibility options.

This information is never sent to us. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the game.

Advertising

The game shows a banner in the main menu, an occasional full-screen ad between levels and optional rewarded videos (for a revive, doubled coins or free coins) through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions such as views, taps and whether a video was finished, and crash diagnostics of the ads SDK.

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings. Ads are requested with a content rating of PG. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases and analytics

This version offers no in-app purchases and contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services of its own. Scores and records stay on your device; there are no online leaderboards.

Links and support

Report a bug and Contact support open your own email app with a message to us. A bug report includes the game version, your device model, Android version, graphics chip, memory size and current level, so we can help. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use what you write only to answer you. Share score opens Android's share sheet with a short text you can send through an app of your choice.

Permissions

The game uses the internet and network-state permissions to load ads, the advertising ID permission for AdMob, and the vibration permission for short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. Gameplay itself does not need the internet. The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos or files.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the game. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

Changes

If the game's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised.
