Crowd Evolution: Army Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Questbyte Labs publishes Crowd Evolution: Army Rush for Android (package com.questbytelabs.crowdevolution). You can play without an account, and gameplay works offline. The game saves your progress on your device, uses Google AdMob to show ads and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Questbyte Labs runs no servers of its own and does not sell your personal information.

What the game saves on your device

The game saves the player name and colour you choose, your levels, stars and era, coins and gems, upgrades, units, weapons, skins and formations, daily rewards, missions, achievements, events, lifetime statistics, your ad choice, your purchases, and settings such as music, sound, vibration, graphics quality and battery saver. The leaderboard is calculated on your device against computer rivals; your scores are not uploaded. Questbyte Labs does not receive a copy of your save.

Ads and privacy choices

The game shows occasional full-screen ads between levels. You can also choose to watch a video for an in-game reward. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, the approximate location it can infer from that address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers such as your Android advertising ID. Google uses this information to show and measure ads, analyse how they work and prevent fraud. The SDK encrypts the information in transit.

No ads are loaded until you confirm the privacy screen on first launch. There you can turn personalised ads off, and you can change that choice at any time under Settings → Personalised ads. When it is off, Google shows only non-personalised ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Gem packs, bundles and Remove Ads are sold through Google Play Billing, using Unity's In-App Purchasing library. Google handles payment, and Questbyte Labs never sees your card or payment details. The game receives a purchase receipt so it can deliver your items and restore them through Settings → Restore Purchases. Google processes purchases under its own privacy policy, and Unity's library may send basic purchase and diagnostic data to Unity Technologies under Unity's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The game uses internet access to load ads and process purchases, the advertising ID permission for AdMob, and vibration for short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos or files.

Keeping and deleting data

To erase your game data, use Settings → Reset Progress, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the game. Google and Unity keep their data under their own privacy policies. If you email us, we use your address and message only to respond, and keep the conversation as long as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to delete that correspondence at the contact address below.

Children

Crowd Evolution: Army Rush is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above.
