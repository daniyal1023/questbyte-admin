Neon Dash: Endless Runner 3D

Privacy policy · Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Neon Dash: Endless Runner 3D (package com.questbytes.neondash), published by Neon Dash Studio, handles your information.

Summary

The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. It works fully offline.

What stays on your device

  • The player name you type in, your progress (coins, gems, unlocked runners, hoverboards and worlds, missions, records) and your settings.
  • These are kept in one save file in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No network connections. The game does not use the internet.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything the game stored.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads or online features), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be shown in the app.
