Privacy Policy

Noor: Prayer Times & Quran · Effective 16 November 2026

This policy covers Noor: Prayer Times & Quran (package com.questbyte.noor), published on Google Play by Questbyte Labs ("the app"). The short version: your location stays on your phone, and the app collects no personal information, shows no ads and has no accounts.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools. We do not receive your name, email, location, contacts, advertising ID or usage data, and we do not share or sell any data with anyone.

Your location

Prayer times and the Qibla direction depend on where you are, so the app asks for your location. You can allow it, or pick your city from a list instead.

  • The prayer times and Qibla are calculated on your phone. Your coordinates are never sent to us or to any server of ours.
  • To show the name of your city, the app asks Android's built-in geocoder to turn your coordinates into a place name. On most phones that service is provided by Google, under Google's privacy policy.
  • The app only reads your location while you are using it (for example when you open it or tap "Use my location"). It never tracks you in the background.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your device only, so it works offline and remembers your choices:

  • Location and settings: your saved coordinates or city, calculation method, Asr method, alert choices, Hijri adjustment, Quran and display preferences.
  • Your worship records: the prayer tracker, Qada counts, tasbih counts, Quran bookmarks, last read position and favourite duas.
  • Quran cache: surahs you have opened or downloaded, so they read offline.

None of this leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all of it.

Network requests

The app downloads the Quran text and translations from alquran.cloud and recitation audio from the Islamic Network audio service (cdn.islamic.network). A request only says which surah, translation or reciter is needed.

These requests contain no personal information. Like any internet connection, they carry technical details such as your IP address, which those services may log briefly for security and operation. We do not use these details to identify or track you.

Permissions

  • Location (approximate or precise, optional): to calculate prayer times and the Qibla for where you are.
  • Notifications (optional, Android 13+): for Azan alerts, pre-prayer reminders, Friday Al-Kahf and morning/evening adhkar reminders. You can turn each one off in the app.
  • Alarms & reminders: so alerts arrive exactly at prayer time.
  • Run at startup: to set the prayer alerts again after your phone restarts or its time zone changes.
  • Vibration: for the tasbih counter and alerts.
  • Internet and network state: to download the Quran and recitations, and to know when you are offline.

The Qibla compass uses your phone's motion sensors while that screen is open. Sensor readings are not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if ads are added, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised before that version is released.
