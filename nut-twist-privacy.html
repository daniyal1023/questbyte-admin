Nut Twist: Bolt Sort Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Questbyte Labs publishes Nut Twist: Bolt Sort Puzzle for Android (package com.questbytes.nuttwist). The game works without an account. It stores your progress on your device and uses Google AdMob to show ads.

Game data on your device

The game saves your chosen player name, level progress, coins, boosters, unlocked items, rewards, scores, and settings. It uses this information to remember where you left off. Questbyte Labs does not receive a copy of your save.

Advertising and consent

The game shows banner and full-screen ads and offers optional videos for in-game rewards. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts this information in transit.

Google's User Messaging Platform shows a privacy message when required. When Google makes privacy choices available, open Settings → Privacy in the game to review or change them. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device for rewards, free spins, and daily challenges. You can turn off these reminders in Android's notification settings for Nut Twist.

Retention and deletion

To remove the game's local data, clear its storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Google retains advertising data under its own privacy policy. If you email us for support, we use your address and message to respond and keep the correspondence as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to delete that correspondence using the contact address below.

Children

Nut Twist is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us to request its removal.
