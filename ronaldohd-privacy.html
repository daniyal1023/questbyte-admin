Privacy Policy

Ronaldo Wallpapers 4K HD · Effective 23 October 2026

This policy covers Ronaldo Wallpapers 4K HD (package com.questbyte.ronaldowallpapers), published on Google Play by Questbyte Labs ("the app"). The short version: we collect no personal information ourselves, and the app has no accounts. The app is free. When it shows ads, they come from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID as described below.

Information we collect

None directly. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or usage data, and we do not sell any data. The only data that can leave your device is what the advertising SDK needs to show ads (next section).

Advertising

The app may show full-screen ads (for example between wallpapers or when you return to the app) served by Google AdMob. To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and technical details about your device. Google uses this to serve, measure and limit ads. How Google handles it is described in Google's advertising policy and privacy policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the app. Anyone can reset or delete their advertising ID in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads. We never receive or store the advertising ID ourselves.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data on your device only, so it works smoothly:

  • Wallpaper catalogue cache: titles, categories and image links, so the grid loads instantly and works offline.
  • Favorites and recently viewed: which wallpapers you have hearted or opened.
  • Settings: grid size, title overlay, and whether new-wallpaper alerts are on.
  • Image cache: downloaded thumbnails and previews.

None of this leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it, and you can clear the image cache, favorites and recently viewed from the app's Settings screen.

Network requests

To show wallpapers, the app downloads the wallpaper list from Supabase and the images from Cloudflare. If new-wallpaper alerts are on, the app checks for new wallpapers in the background now and then.

These requests contain no personal information. Like any internet connection, they carry technical details such as your IP address, which the hosting providers may log briefly for security and operation. We do not use these details to identify or track you.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download wallpapers and to know when you are offline.
  • Set wallpaper: to apply an image to your home or lock screen when you ask.
  • Notifications (optional, Android 13+): to tell you when new wallpapers are added. You can decline it, or turn alerts off in Settings at any time.

Downloaded wallpapers are saved to your Pictures/Wallpapers folder through Android's media store, so the app never needs access to your other photos or files.

Wallpaper content

This is an unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Cristiano Ronaldo or any other player, club, league, federation or sponsor. Names are used only to describe the wallpapers. If you own an image in the app and want it credited or removed, email us at the address below and we will act on it promptly.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Ads are requested with Google's "not directed at children" setting.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised before that version is released.
