Privacy Policy

Game Box Arcade · Effective 29 September 2026

This policy covers the game Game Box Arcade (package com.questbytes.GameBoxArcade), published by Questbyte Labs ("the game"). The short version: the game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and it does not use the internet.

Information we collect

None. The game never asks for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. No data leaves your device.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps a small amount of information on your device only:

  • Scores and stars: your best score and stars for each mini game.
  • Levels: which level you have reached in the puzzle games.
  • Settings: whether sound and music are on.

This information is never sent anywhere. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Reset Progress, and uninstalling the game deletes it completely.

Advertising and purchases

The game shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. It does not use your device's advertising ID. If this ever changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Permissions

The game does not request any special permissions. It does not access the internet, your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children

Game Box Arcade is designed to be enjoyed by children and families. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Parents are welcome to contact us with any questions.

Third parties

The game is built with the Unity engine. No Unity services that collect data are enabled, and no other third-party services are included. Downloads and updates are handled by Google Play under the Google privacy policy.

Changes

If the game's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised.
