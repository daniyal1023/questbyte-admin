Curve King: Free Kick Football Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Questbyte Labs publishes Curve King: Free Kick Football for Android (package com.questbytes.curveking). You can play without creating an account. The game saves your progress on your device and uses Google AdMob to show ads.

What the game saves on your device

The game saves your chosen player name and avatar, career progress, scores, coins, power-ups, unlocked items, rewards, and settings. This lets you pick up where you left off. Questbyte Labs does not receive a copy of your save.

Ads and privacy choices

Curve King shows banner and full-screen ads. You can also choose to watch a video for an in-game reward or to revive a run. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, the approximate location it can infer from that address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers such as your Android advertising ID. Google uses this information to show and measure ads, analyze how they work, and prevent fraud. The SDK encrypts the information in transit.

Google's User Messaging Platform displays a privacy message when required. When Google offers privacy choices in the app, open Settings → Privacy to review or change them. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device for rewards, free spins, daily challenges, and returning to play. You can turn the reminders off in Android's notification settings for Curve King.

Keeping and deleting data

To remove the game's local data, clear Curve King's app storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Google retains its data under its own privacy policy. If you email us, we use your address and message to respond and keep the conversation as needed to handle your request. You can use the contact address below to ask us to delete that correspondence.

Children

Curve King is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us to ask for its removal.
