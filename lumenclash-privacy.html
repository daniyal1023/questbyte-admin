Lumen Clash: One Button Fight

Privacy policy · Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Lumen Clash: One Button Fight (package com.questbytes.lumenclash), published by Questbyte Labs, handles your information. The short version: we do not sell your information, and we do not ask for your real name, email, contacts, photos or location. There are no accounts and no sign-up, every opponent is a computer fighter, and your progress stays on your phone. The game is free to play and shows ads from Google AdMob.

What stays on your device

  • The player name you choose, your progress (coins, gems, level, unlocked fighters, auras and arenas, Tower floor, ranked league standing, missions, daily rewards, achievements and records) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, screen shake, daily reminders, button size, graphics and difficulty).
  • These are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never sent to us.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the home, modes, shop and results screens (never during a fight), an occasional full-screen ad when you leave the results of a fight (at most once every two fights and never more than once every two minutes), and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward: double the coins from a fight, or free coins or gems in the shop. Rewards are only given after Google confirms the video was watched.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this information to show ads based on your interests (personalised ads). Ads are limited to content suitable for teens (T) and younger audiences. Google handles this information under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google privacy policy. We never receive this information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game sets local reminders on your phone (for example when your daily reward or the new Daily Trial is ready). They are scheduled by the game itself, contain no ads and send nothing to us or anyone else. You can turn them off in Settings › Daily reminders in the game, or in your Android notification settings.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): in these regions the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the Privacy button in the game's Settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings under Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads on some devices).
  • Reward videos are always optional. Every fighter, aura, arena and mode can be unlocked and played without watching one.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in, and no online play or chat with other people.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting of our own.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Permissions

The game uses the internet and network state for ads, the advertising ID permission for ads, vibration for short haptic feedback (which you can turn off in Settings), and the notification and "run at startup" permissions so your daily reminders still arrive after your phone restarts. Fights can be played offline; ads simply don't load without a connection.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's data in your Android settings (Settings › Apps › Lumen Clash › Storage › Clear data) or uninstall the app. Either removes everything the game stored. We hold no copy to delete.

Children

Lumen Clash is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the game. Ad traffic to Google is encrypted (HTTPS). Google keeps its data according to its own retention policies.

Changes

If the game's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if online play is added, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
