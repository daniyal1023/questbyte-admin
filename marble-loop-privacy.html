Marble Loop: Color Sort Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Questbyte Labs publishes Marble Loop: Color Sort Puzzle for Android (package com.questbytes.marbleloop). You can play without an account. The game saves your progress on your device and uses Google AdMob to show ads.

Game data on your device

The game saves your chosen name and avatar, level progress, Marble Rush records, daily challenge streak, coins, boosters, unlocked items, rewards, and settings. It uses this information to remember your progress. Questbyte Labs does not receive a copy of your save.

Ads and privacy choices

The game shows banner and full-screen ads and offers optional videos for in-game rewards. Google AdMob may collect and share your IP address, approximate location inferred from that address, app interactions, diagnostics, and identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts this information in transit.

Google's SDK may also collect the Android App Set ID for analytics and fraud prevention. Questbyte Labs does not use the App Set ID to personalize or measure ads or link it to advertising IDs or personal data for advertising.

Google's User Messaging Platform displays a privacy message when required. When Google makes privacy choices available, open Settings → Privacy in the game to review them. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Daily reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules daily reminders on your device about levels, challenges, and rewards. Android's startup permission lets the game restore these reminders after your device restarts. You can turn them off in Android's notification settings for Marble Loop.

Keeping and deleting data

To remove local game data from your device, clear Marble Loop's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. Google retains its data under its own privacy policy. If you email us, we use your address and message to respond and keep the conversation as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to delete that correspondence using the contact address below.

Children

Marble Loop is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us to ask for its removal.
