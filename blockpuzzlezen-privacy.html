Privacy Policy

Block Puzzle Zen · Effective 9 November 2026

This policy covers the game Block Puzzle Zen (package com.questbytes.blockpuzzlezen), published by Questbyte Labs ("the game", "we"). The short version: we do not collect, store on our servers, or sell any personal information. The game has no sign-up and no in-app purchases, and your progress stays on your phone. The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to deliver those ads (see Advertising below).

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress and preferences, the game keeps the following on your device only:

  • Scores and statistics: your best score, games played, lines cleared, best combo, perfect clears and total score (which sets your level and rank).
  • Daily challenge progress: which days you completed and your streak.
  • Trophies: your progress towards each trophy and which ones you have unlocked.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration and your chosen theme, plus whether you have seen the how-to-play guide.
  • An unfinished game, so you can continue it later.

This information is never sent to us. You can erase your progress from Settings → Reset progress, and uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a second chance when you run out of moves. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app details, and how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped). Google may use this information to show ads based on your interests (personalised ads). Google handles this information under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google privacy policy. We never receive or see this information.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): in these regions the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads on some devices). You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.
  • Vibration: you can turn haptic feedback off in the game's settings.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device and stops all data collection by the game and Google AdMob.

Permissions

The game uses the internet and network state so Google AdMob can load ads, and vibration for short haptic feedback. Ads include the advertising ID permission. The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos or files. The game itself works without an internet connection.

Children

Block Puzzle Zen is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the game in a way that resulted in information being collected, please contact us and we will help you resolve it.

Third parties

The game is built with the Unity engine. No Unity services that collect data are enabled. The only third-party service included is Google AdMob for ads, including Google's User Messaging Platform for consent. Downloads and updates are handled by Google Play under the Google privacy policy.

Data security and retention

Progress data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google AdMob is sent over encrypted connections and kept according to Google's retention policies.

Changes

If the game's behaviour changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised.
