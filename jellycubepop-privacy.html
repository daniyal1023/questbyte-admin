Privacy Policy

Jelly Cube Pop · Effective 28 September 2026

This policy covers the game Jelly Cube Pop (package com.questbytes.JellyCubePop), published by Questbyte Labs ("the game"). The short version: we do not collect your personal information ourselves, the game has no accounts and shows no ads. The game uses Unity Analytics to understand how it is played, and Google Play handles any purchases, as described below.

Information we collect

None directly. The game has no sign-in and does not ask for your email, contacts, photos or precise location. The only data that leaves your device is what the two services below need to work.

Analytics

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity Analytics (by Unity Technologies) to measure basic usage, such as how many people play, session length, the app version and crashes or errors. To do this Unity may collect a random device identifier generated by Unity, technical details about your device (model, operating system, language, screen size), approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address, and in-game events such as when a session starts and ends. This data is used only to improve the game. It is not used for advertising and we do not sell it. How Unity handles it is described in Unity's player privacy policy, which also explains how to request access to or deletion of the data Unity holds.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional coin packs. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google shares only an order confirmation with the game so your coins can be delivered, and basic purchase events (which pack and when) may be included in the analytics above. Google's handling of payments is described in the Google privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps a small amount of data on your device only, so it works smoothly and offline:

  • Player profile: the name and avatar you choose on first launch. The name can be anything, it is only shown back to you, and it never leaves your device.
  • Progress: unlocked levels, stars, best scores and the last level you played.
  • Game items: coins, lives and boosters.
  • Daily gift streak: the date of your last daily reward.
  • Settings: sound, music and similar preferences.

Uninstalling the game deletes all of this. You can also clear your progress at any time from Settings → Reset progress and change your name from the home screen.

Advertising

The game currently shows no ads and does not use your device's advertising ID. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Permissions

  • Internet: used for Unity Analytics and Google Play purchases. The game itself is fully playable offline.
  • Google Play billing: to offer optional in-app purchases.

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children

Jelly Cube Pop is a family-friendly puzzle game rated for all ages, but it is not directed primarily at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and deletion

Data sent to Unity and Google is encrypted in transit. Because we hold no data ourselves, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To delete the analytics data associated with your device, email us and we will pass the request to Unity, or use the options in Unity's policy linked above.

Changes

If the game's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised.
